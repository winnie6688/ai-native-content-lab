# wechat-fact-checker

WeChat fact-checking Skill mirror for `要AI不释手`.

## Role

Verify technical, financial, regulatory, market, and current AI claims before publication.

## Current change impact

The fact-checking logic remains mostly unchanged, but the scope should include:

- AI product trends
- enterprise AI / To B AI
- AI impact on knowledge work
- AI impact on labor markets
- tools, platforms, APIs, agents, and workflow capabilities

## Rules

- Prefer official and authoritative sources.
- Distinguish fact, interpretation, assumption, and personal experience.
- Do not overstate AI capability.
- For finance/investment content, avoid recommendations and market predictions.
